Privacy Policy Details.

Last updated: September 24, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information TUBAL HUB may handle when you use the website and its connected features.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, TUBAL HUB may receive account information such as your name, email address, profile information, authentication identifiers, and information you choose to add to your account. Community features may also receive messages, comments, reactions, profile content, and other information you submit.

2. Authentication

TUBAL HUB uses Firebase Authentication for supported sign-in methods. Authentication providers may process information needed to verify your account and provide secure sign-in. We do not ask you to provide passwords through TUBAL HUB's public pages.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to provide accounts and profiles, operate Global Chat and community features, personalize settings, maintain security, moderate abuse, respond to support requests, improve features, and operate the website.

4. Community and public information

Information you intentionally publish in public or community areas may be visible to other users. Avoid posting passwords, financial information, private contact details, or other sensitive information in public chats, profiles, comments, or other community features.

5. Moderation and safety

To protect the community, TUBAL HUB may process moderation-related information such as warnings, chat status, mute or ban status, moderation reasons, and moderation timestamps. This information may be used to enforce the Terms of Use and protect the service.

6. Device and technical information

Depending on the service and browser, technical information may be processed by TUBAL HUB or its service providers, such as browser type, device information, IP-related information, timestamps, error information, and basic request information. Some information may be handled automatically by hosting, authentication, security, or other infrastructure providers.

7. Cookies and browser storage

TUBAL HUB may use cookies, local storage, IndexedDB, and related browser technologies for authentication, security, theme preferences, interface settings, and application functionality. See the Cookies Policy for more details.

8. Firebase and service providers

Parts of TUBAL HUB rely on third-party infrastructure, including Firebase services. These providers may process information on our behalf or as part of their own services. Their own privacy terms may also apply.

9. AI features

TUBAL HUB may provide AI or automated features. Questions or content entered into those features may be processed to generate a response. Do not enter passwords, financial information, or highly sensitive personal information into AI prompts.

10. Links and third-party services

The website may contain links or integrations for Discord, social networks, news sources, payment services, or other external providers. When you leave TUBAL HUB or interact with an external service, that provider's privacy policy and terms may apply.

11. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or enforce our terms. Actual retention periods may vary by data type and service provider.

12. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the features we operate. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and report suspicious activity.

13. Your choices

Where available, you may update profile information, adjust settings, clear browser storage, stop using a feature, or contact TUBAL HUB about privacy questions. Some information may need to be retained for security, legal, or operational reasons.

14. Children's privacy

TUBAL HUB is not intended to bypass age or consent requirements. Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for a minor to use a particular service, that requirement still applies.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our features, services, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

16. Contact

For privacy questions, contact YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL or use the TUBAL HUB Contact page.

Important: This is a general website privacy-policy draft, not legal advice. The final policy should be reviewed against the actual Firebase configuration, hosting, analytics, payment, advertising, AI, and other services used by TUBAL HUB and the laws applicable to the site's users.

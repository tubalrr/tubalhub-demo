TUBAL HUB

How to Use

A practical guide to the main TUBAL HUB features.

Create or update your profile from Profiles or Settings. Use Feeds to publish community posts, react and comment. Open Payapang Isip for your local journal and breathing tools. AI Music stores generated tracks in this browser, while CTRLZONE keeps gameplay data available on this device.

How to Post

Open Feeds, use the post composer, add your content, then publish. Review your post before submitting.

How to Delete Post

Open your published post from Feeds and use the delete control provided by the page. Deleted content should no longer be displayed after the local or server operation completes.

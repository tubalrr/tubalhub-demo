HOW COOKIES ARE USED

Cookies Policy Details.

Last updated: September 24, 2026. This Cookies Policy explains how TUBAL HUB may use cookies, local storage, and similar browser technologies to provide and improve the website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. They can help a website remember preferences, maintain sessions, improve security, and understand how features are used.

2. Cookies and local storage we may use

TUBAL HUB may use browser storage technologies for authentication state, theme preferences, interface settings, chat and application functionality, security-related state, and remembering choices made on the site. Some features may use local storage rather than traditional cookies.

3. Essential technologies

Some storage is necessary for core functionality. For example, authentication and security features may need browser storage so that signed-in sessions and required preferences can work correctly. Disabling essential storage may cause parts of TUBAL HUB to stop working properly.

4. Preference cookies and storage

We may remember choices such as your selected visual theme, automatic theme preference, and other interface settings so you do not need to configure them every time you visit.

5. Authentication and Firebase

TUBAL HUB uses Firebase services for parts of its authentication and application functionality. Firebase may use cookies, local storage, IndexedDB, or related browser mechanisms as part of providing those services. The specific technologies used can depend on the Firebase feature and browser.

6. Third-party services

Some pages or features may connect to third-party services such as Google/Firebase, Discord, social-media platforms, hosting services, or other external providers. Those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

7. Analytics and advertising

TUBAL HUB does not represent that every visit is free from third-party technologies. If analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other tracking services are introduced, this policy should be updated to identify the relevant provider, purpose, categories of data, and retention or control options.

8. How to control cookies

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also clear site data, local storage, and cached data. Blocking all storage may affect login, themes, chat, and other TUBAL HUB features.

9. Browser choices

Most modern browsers provide controls for allowing, blocking, deleting, or limiting cookies. The available controls differ between browsers and devices, so check your browser's privacy or site-settings menu for the current options.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy when the website's technologies or services change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about cookies or browser storage used by TUBAL HUB, contact us at YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL or through our Discord community.
